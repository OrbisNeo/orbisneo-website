This week is about oversight that has not yet been assigned to anyone. Australia plans to put safety requirements for frontier AI companies into law. Three studies, two from organisations with a commercial interest in the topic, paint a similar picture: boards discuss AI, but who is accountable, which AI systems are in use and how fast an agent can be stopped is often not settled.
