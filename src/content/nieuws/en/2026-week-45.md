This week is about agents you can still steer. The UK is preparing rules for loss of control. MIT Technology Review contrasts connectors with computer use and describes mechanism design as another way to govern agents. Intuit shows what can break when agents lack shared context. Meanwhile politics is slowing data centre buildout.
