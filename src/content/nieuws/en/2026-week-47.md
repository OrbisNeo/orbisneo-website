This week is about keeping track of AI, and that responsibility sits with the board itself. Brussels shows which questions the fundamental rights impact assessment will ask. New Zealand reminds businesses that existing law already applies and that AI does not shift accountability. In the US, disclosure of Chinese models is on the table, and the UK's FRC warns that AI insights from auditors can threaten their independence. If you do not know where AI is used in your organisation and who is accountable for it, you cannot answer any of these questions.
