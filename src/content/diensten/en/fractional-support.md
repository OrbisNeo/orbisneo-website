### When do you need this?

The direction is set, but you temporarily lack the senior capacity to organise and deliver it, and a permanent hire does not fit.

### What do we do?

We agree an assignment with concrete deliverables, a fixed rhythm and an endpoint. I work inside your organisation on delivery and then hand over to an internal owner.

### What do you get?

A fixed rhythm, direct access, periodic reporting to the leadership team, and at the end a handover to an internal owner.

### Where my background counts

IT delivery, IT operations and integration programmes.
