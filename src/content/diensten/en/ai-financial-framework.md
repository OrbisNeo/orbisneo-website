### When do you need this?

You want to know what AI really costs, and when an investment is defensible, before you scale further.

### What do we look at?

The cost of the applications, the expected benefits, the assumptions behind them and the points where you adjust or stop.

### What do you get?

A framework and financial model with assumptions, scenarios and decision points, plus a plan to embed it in your existing planning and control cycle.
