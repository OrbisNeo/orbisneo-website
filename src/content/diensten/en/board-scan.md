### When do you need this?

AI initiatives are running or planned, but there is no clear overall picture. What do they deliver, who owns them and where should you start?

### What do we look at?

I map current and planned initiatives and their effect on IT, people and cost, and where ownership or information is missing.

### What do you get?

A leadership conversation and a concise priorities memo: where you stand, the priorities, what information is still missing and which decisions come next.
