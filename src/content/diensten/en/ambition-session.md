### When do you need this?

Your leadership team holds different views of what AI should mean for your IT and organisation: cost savings, innovation or "something we cannot afford to miss". Until there is a shared choice, initiatives stay disconnected.

### What do we decide?

The ambition for AI in IT and organisation, the boundaries (what AI is and is not used for), the investment criteria and who owns it.

### What do you get?

An agreed position document with those decisions, ready for internal use.

### Where my background counts

Experience with governance and decision criteria at group level.
